# Explorables

Interactive simulations and visual explanations of physics, mathematics and complex systems. Each top-level folder is
one project with its own README, tests and license, and each runs in the browser with no build step.

Gallery: https://fushanbobfan.github.io/

| Project | What it shows | Folder |
|---|---|---|
| [iris](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/iris/) | Rainbows in the browser: follow sunlight through a raindrop, find the ray where light piles up, and see the primary bow, the reversed secondary and the dark band between them. | [`iris/`](iris/) |
| [linkwork](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/linkwork/) | A four-bar linkage lab in the browser: drag the pivots and joints, turn the crank, and watch a point on the coupler trace loops, figure eights and nearly straight lines. | [`linkwork/`](linkwork/) |
| [drape](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/drape/) | Cloth in the browser: hang a curtain, a banner or a flag from pins, grab, cut and tear it, and watch every thread coloured by how hard it is pulled. | [`drape/`](drape/) |
| [cycloid](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/cycloid/) | The brachistochrone and the tautochrone in the browser: race beads from A to B along a ramp, an arc, the cycloid and a track you draw, then drop them into a cycloidal bowl where every start takes the same time. | [`cycloid/`](cycloid/) |
| [fatou](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/fatou/) | The Mandelbrot set and its Julia sets side by side: pick c, follow the orbit of 0 and see which cycle it settles on. | [`fatou/`](fatou/) |
| [orbium](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/orbium/) | Lenia in the browser: a continuous cellular automaton that grows gliding creatures. Place Orbium and its relatives, paint, and retune the rule live. | [`orbium/`](orbium/) |
| [streakline](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/streakline/) | A wind tunnel in the browser: lattice Boltzmann flow past a cylinder, plate or wing, with vortex streets, streaklines and drawable walls. | [`streakline/`](streakline/) |
| [bowshock](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/bowshock/) | The Doppler effect and the Mach cone in the browser: drag a listener, push the source past the speed of sound, and hear the boom and the backwards sound inside the cone. | [`bowshock/`](bowshock/) |
| [jostle](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/jostle/) | A hard-disk gas in the browser: elastic disks collide in a box, their speeds settle into the Maxwell-Boltzmann curve and the wall pressure follows the hard-disk equation of state. | [`jostle/`](jostle/) |
| [tonnetz](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/tonnetz/) | The Tonnetz in the browser: every triangle a triad, neo-Riemannian P/L/R moves, progressions traced as paths and voice leading you can hear. | [`tonnetz/`](tonnetz/) |
| [attractorium](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/attractorium/) | Strange attractors in the browser: turn Lorenz, Rössler and five relatives in 3D and watch a speck of nearby starts spread over the attractor at the Lyapunov rate. | [`attractorium/`](attractorium/) |
| [kilim](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/kilim/) | Wave function collapse in the browser: paint a small sample and watch a larger pattern woven from its local rules, one cell at a time. | [`kilim/`](kilim/) |
| [dotwork](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/dotwork/) | Weighted Voronoi stippling in the browser: dots settle by Lloyd relaxation until their spacing draws the picture, then one continuous line visits them all. | [`dotwork/`](dotwork/) |
| [plasmodium](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/plasmodium/) | A slime mould in the browser: particles that sense, turn, step and leave a trail grow transport networks between food. | [`plasmodium/`](plasmodium/) |
| [turmite](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/turmite/) | Langton's ant and its many-coloured relatives in the browser: one turning rule per colour, long stretches of chaos, and the highways, squares and triangles that come out of it. | [`turmite/`](turmite/) |
| [gnomon](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/gnomon/) | A sundial and sun-path lab in the browser: where the sun stands for any place, date and time, the shadow it casts, and why sundials and clocks disagree. | [`gnomon/`](gnomon/) |
| [enclave](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/enclave/) | Schelling's segregation model in the browser: households happy as a minority still sort a mixed town into enclaves. | [`enclave/`](enclave/) |
| [quincunx](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/quincunx/) | A Galton board in the browser: drop balls through rows of pegs and watch the bell curve build up, checked against the binomial and normal distributions. | [`quincunx/`](quincunx/) |
| [seepage](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/seepage/) | Percolation in the browser: random grids, merging clusters and the spanning threshold. | [`seepage/`](seepage/) |
| [fivefold](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/fivefold/) | Penrose tilings in the browser: grow kites and darts or rhombi by substitution and watch the golden ratio appear in the tile counts. | [`fivefold/`](fivefold/) |
| [ebru](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/ebru/) | Paper marbling in the browser: float drops of ink, rake them with combs and swirls, and print the pattern. | [`ebru/`](ebru/) |
| [fieldlines](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/fieldlines/) | Electric field lines and equipotentials in the browser: place and drag charges, watch the field reshape, and find the points where it vanishes. | [`fieldlines/`](fieldlines/) |
| [tunnelwell](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/tunnelwell/) | A quantum wave packet in the browser: fire it at barriers, steps, wells and lattices, watch it split, and compare the measured transmission with the exact answer. | [`tunnelwell/`](tunnelwell/) |
| [tendril](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/tendril/) | An L-system garden in the browser: write rewriting rules, grow plants and fractal curves with turtle graphics, and watch them unfold stroke by stroke. | [`tendril/`](tendril/) |
| [lampyrid](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/lampyrid/) | Fireflies that learn to flash together: the Kuramoto model of coupled oscillators in the browser, checked live against the exact synchronization threshold. | [`lampyrid/`](lampyrid/) |
| [topple](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/topple/) | An Abelian sandpile in the browser: pour towers, rain grains and watch avalanches, and find the sandpile group identity. | [`topple/`](topple/) |
| [hoarfrost](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/hoarfrost/) | Grow snow crystals in the browser with Reiter's hexagonal cellular model. | [`hoarfrost/`](hoarfrost/) |
| [prismatic](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/prismatic/) | A 2D optics bench in the browser: trace light through prisms, lenses and mirrors, with Fresnel splitting, total internal reflection and glass dispersion. | [`prismatic/`](prismatic/) |
| [curiepoint](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/curiepoint/) | The 2D Ising model in the browser: cool a lattice of magnetic spins through its critical temperature and watch order appear. | [`curiepoint/`](curiepoint/) |
| [sandsong](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/sandsong/) | A browser Chladni plate: sweep a tone and watch sand gather on the nodal lines of a vibrating square plate. | [`sandsong/`](sandsong/) |
| [harmonograph](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/harmonograph/) | A browser harmonograph: damped pendulums steer a pen and trace slowly turning, decaying Lissajous figures you can tune, share and export. | [`harmonograph/`](harmonograph/) |
| [phantomjam](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/phantomjam/) | An interactive traffic-jam lab: phantom jams on a ring road, a space-time diagram and a flow-density curve you map yourself. | [`phantomjam/`](phantomjam/) |
| [pendulab](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/pendulab/) | An interactive double-pendulum chaos lab: release a fan of nearly identical pendulums and watch tiny differences grow into completely different motion. | [`pendulab/`](pendulab/) |
| [morphogen](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/morphogen/) | An interactive reaction-diffusion pattern lab: paint seeds, tune feed and kill rates, and watch spots, stripes, corals and worms grow in the browser. | [`morphogen/`](morphogen/) |
| [ripplefield](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/ripplefield/) | An interactive ripple tank: drop wave sources, draw walls and watch interference, diffraction, reflection and refraction in the browser. | [`ripplefield/`](ripplefield/) |
| [murmuration](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/murmuration/) | An interactive flocking simulation: tune the rules that steer hundreds of boids and watch a flock emerge. | [`murmuration/`](murmuration/) |
| [epicyclon](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/epicyclon/) | Draw a shape and watch a chain of rotating circles retrace it with the discrete Fourier transform. | [`epicyclon/`](epicyclon/) |
| [pathlight](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/pathlight/) | An interactive pathfinding visualizer for grid search algorithms. | [`pathlight/`](pathlight/) |
| [chromalens](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/chromalens/) | An interactive color-vision-deficiency simulator for images. | [`chromalens/`](chromalens/) |
| [gravity-garden](https://fushanbobfan.github.io/gravity-garden/) | An interactive N-body gravity simulator for exploring orbital mechanics in the browser. | [`gravity-garden/`](gravity-garden/) |
