cusp

A wheel, a pin and two rubber bands. Move the free end of one band a little and the wheel turns a little, until, with no extra effort from you, it snaps round. Move back the way you came and it does not snap back until much later. All of this is decided by one curved diamond in the plane your hand moves in.
