Hoarfrost

Grow a snow crystal from a single frozen cell. Vapour drifts between the cells of a hexagonal grid, the edge of the ice catches it, and two numbers decide whether you get a plate, a star or a fern.

Keys: Space pauses and resumes, N steps, R restarts.
