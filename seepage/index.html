Seepage

Percolation on a square grid. Open each cell with probability p and neighbouring open cells join into clusters. Below about 0.593 they stay small; just above it one cluster suddenly reaches from edge to edge.

Keyboard: with the grid focused, ← and → nudge p by 0.001 (hold Shift for 0.01), T jumps to this grid's threshold, N draws a new grid, B switches between site and bond percolation, C cycles the colouring and F lights the top row or puts the fire out.
