dotwork

Scatter dots where a picture is dark, then move each dot to the ink-weighted centre of the patch of page closest to it, again and again. The dots spread out evenly and their spacing alone draws the picture. When they settle, one unbroken line can be threaded through all of them.

Drop an image on the page or choose one below. Space runs or pauses, R reseeds, L draws the line, P plays the plot.
