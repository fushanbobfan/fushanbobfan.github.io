An electron in a hydrogen atom has no path. It has a cloud: the chance of finding it at each point, given by the square of its wavefunction ψ. Pick an orbital and see that cloud as electrons caught one at a time, as a cut through the nucleus, and as a chart of how far from the nucleus the electron sits.

The curve is r²R(r)², the chance per unit distance of finding the electron at radius r; it takes the r² because a shell at radius r has more room in it than one near the centre. The bars count the dots in the cloud by distance.
