Pathlight

An interactive pathfinding visualizer for grid search algorithms.

20ms

Click or drag across empty cells to draw with the active tool (walls, or weighted terrain that costs more to cross); click a cell already drawn on to erase it. Use Place start… / Place end…, then click a cell, to move either marker. Breadth-First Search treats every step as equally costly, so it ignores weighted terrain entirely — Dijkstra and A* are the ones that route around it. The grid is keyboard-operable too: focus it and use the arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to draw, erase, or place on the focused cell.

Grid search visualized step by step, from the algorithm's own exploration order.
