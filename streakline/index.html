streakline

Air enters on the left and flows past an obstacle. At low Reynolds numbers the flow closes up neatly behind it; raise the number and the wake starts to swing from side to side, shedding eddies of alternating spin into a vortex street. The dye lines are streaklines: everything ever released from one point near the inlet.

Obstacle

The size is the diameter, side, plate length or chord, in lattice cells, and it is the length in the Reynolds number. A positive angle raises the leading edge.

Re = U D / ν. The viscosity ν sets how fast the lattice relaxes; below τ = 0.51 the scheme goes unstable, so a Reynolds number too high for the obstacle size is capped there. A larger obstacle or a faster inflow reaches higher.
