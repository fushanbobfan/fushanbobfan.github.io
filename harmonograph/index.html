Pendulums

Keys: Space pause · R replay · F finish · N surprise me

A harmonograph hangs a pen and a drawing table from pendulums. Each pendulum swings as A·sin(2πf t + φ)·e−d t; the pen follows the sum. Frequencies near a whole-number ratio make closed Lissajous figures, the tiny mismatch makes them turn, and friction draws them inward.
