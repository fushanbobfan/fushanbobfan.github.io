Fivefold

Penrose tilings, grown by hand. Start from a sun or a star, cut every tile into smaller kites and darts or rhombi, and repeat. The pattern covers the plane without ever repeating, and the ratio of the two tiles closes in on the golden ratio.

Keyboard: with the tiling focused, + and − step the generation, T switches between kites and darts and rhombi, S switches between sun and star, C cycles the colouring, and O outlines tiles one more generation back.
