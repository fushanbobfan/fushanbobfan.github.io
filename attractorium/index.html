attractorium

Three equations, no randomness, and still no way to say where the orbit will be a minute from now. Release a cloud of starting points closer together than a speck of dust and watch the flow pull them apart over the whole attractor, at the rate the largest Lyapunov exponent predicts.

Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom. Space runs or pauses, R releases a new cloud, O shows or hides the orbit, arrow keys turn, + and − zoom.
